How a launch runs

مراحل الإطلاق
  1. PHASE 1 · TEASER

    Build anticipation

    التشويق

    Name and identity, presenter reveals, social channels live before air, first creator and press partnerships.

  2. PHASE 2 · LAUNCH

    Go on air everywhere at once

    الإطلاق

    Satellite, app and FAST live on the same day, backed by the main media buy, outdoor in key cities and a launch event.

  3. PHASE 3 · SUSTAIN

    Turn viewers into a habit

    الاستمرارية

    Always-on social video, performance media for app installs, and monthly optimisation against the numbers.

WHAT WE MEASURE
  • Reach & video views
  • App installs & active users
  • Followers by platform
  • Live & FAST viewing
  • Cost per result
